About Trade Hunter

Trade Hunter is a business lead discovery and prospecting platform developed to help business owners, entrepreneurs, sales professionals and organisations discover and organise potential business leads.

The platform provides digital tools that can help users identify potential businesses, customers and business opportunities relevant to their target market.

Trade Hunter is designed to support the process of finding, organising and researching potential prospects before initiating business communication or customer outreach.

Purpose of Trade Hunter

Trade Hunter helps businesses discover potential leads and organise prospect information so that users can conduct their own business research and customer-development activities more efficiently.

How Trade Hunter Works

Trade Hunter is designed around a simple business prospecting workflow:

  1. Define your target market — identify the type of businesses, customers or prospects you are looking for.
  2. Discover potential leads — use the available search and discovery functionality to identify relevant prospects.
  3. Review information — evaluate the information available about potential leads.
  4. Organise prospects — maintain relevant prospect information for your own business-development workflow.
  5. Conduct your own outreach — decide independently how and when to contact potential customers in accordance with applicable laws and communication requirements.

Users should independently verify important information before making business decisions or contacting a prospect.
