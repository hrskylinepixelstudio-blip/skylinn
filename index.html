FAQ

Common questions

How long does a typical website take?+

A business website typically ships in 2–3 weeks. Full web applications with custom backends run 4–10 weeks depending on scope.

Do you work with businesses outside Chennai?+

Yes — we work with clients across Tamil Nadu and remotely across India. Most collaboration happens over calls and WhatsApp.

What does ongoing support include?+

Bug fixes, minor content updates and uptime monitoring are included post-launch. Larger feature work is scoped separately.

Can you take over an existing site or platform?+

Yes, including migrations off no-code platforms onto owned, lower-cost infrastructure like Cloudflare.

Do you offer digital marketing without a rebuild?+

Yes — SEO, ads and social media management are offered standalone or alongside a build.
